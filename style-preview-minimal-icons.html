Which visual direction feels right for answer options?

These are understated line icons rather than emoji. They keep the quiz scannable while preserving the premium, minimal tone.

Layered abstract symbol Feels elevated and design-forward without reading playful.
Landscape line icon Works well for abstract and visual question prompts.
Minimal symbolic icons Readable and elegant if you want a little more structure.
